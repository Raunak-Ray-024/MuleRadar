What MuleRadar does

One workspace for scoring, ring discovery and case review.

Score any account

An XGBoost model reads each account's payment history and returns a risk score against a tuned threshold.

Find whole rings

Graph analysis groups accounts that move money together, so you review one ring instead of many single accounts.

Explained case reports

An AI agent applies RBI and NPCI-style rules and writes the case. An admin approves or rejects it.

See the ring, not just the node

Mule rings pass money through many accounts in quick hops. MuleRadar links these accounts so an investigator sees the full pattern in one view.

Rules that always apply

  • Receiving money never needs your UPI PIN. If an app asks for it, you are paying.
  • Check the name your UPI app shows before you pay.
  • Never share an OTP, PIN or CVV with anyone.
  • Be careful with "refund", "KYC update" and "easy task" offers.

Already lost money?

Call 1930 straight away and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Then tell your bank. Reporting quickly gives the best chance of stopping the money.
